# 🐔 Poultry Hub Kenya

> **Kenya's Premier Poultry Marketplace** - Connecting farmers, vendors,
> and customers across Kenya

## 🎯 The Problem We're Solving

The Kenyan poultry market faces critical challenges:

- **Fragmented Market**: Farmers and customers lack a centralized platform to connect
- **Quality Trust Issues**: Difficulty verifying product authenticity and quality
- **Geographic Barriers**: Limited access to suppliers outside local areas
- **Market Information Gap**: Lack of clear pricing and product availability information
- **Payment Friction**: Unsafe and cumbersome transaction methods

## 💡 Our Solution

**Poultry Hub Kenya** is a digital marketplace platform that:

- Brings together poultry farmers, vendors, and customers on one trusted platform
- Uses AI to verify product quality through image analysis
- Enables location-based discovery of products and suppliers across Kenya
- Provides secure payment processing and order tracking
- Offers a multi-language experience (English & Kiswahili) to serve all users
- Helps vendors reach more customers through advertisements and better visibility

## ✨ What We've Built

- **Product Marketplace**: Browse, search, and purchase poultry products by location,
 and category
- **Vendor Profiles**: Verified farmer and supplier profiles with location information
- **Shopping & Orders**: Secure checkout and order tracking from purchase to delivery
- **AI Product Verification**: Google Gemini-powered image analysis for product
 quality assurance
- **Smart Chatbot**: Chatbase-powered assistant that answers questions in English
 and Kiswahili
- **Advertisement System**: Vendors can promote products to reach more customers
- **Admin Dashboard**: Platform management and vendor verification tools

## 🛠️ Built With

- **Frontend**: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: PHP, MySQL, Apache
- **AI**: Google Gemini (image analysis), Chatbase (conversational AI)
